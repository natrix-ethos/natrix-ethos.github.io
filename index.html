How it actually works

Three pieces.
You place zero trades.

    The strategy

    A pre-built algorithm watches the chart and decides when to act. You don't build it or predict anything. You learn to read and trust the data behind it.

Do I need my own money to trade?

Not your own trading capital, no. You trade on a funded account that a prop firm provides. Your own cost is the small entry fee for their evaluation, plus the one-time €79 for this course.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You're given working, pre-built strategies. The course teaches you to set them up, read the data, and connect the pieces. There's no coding and no advanced math anywhere in it.

How much time does it take to run?

Setting it up is the main effort, and the course walks you through every step. After that it's minutes a day. Roughly a daily check-in, not hours at a screen.

Is this actually automated, or do I place trades?

It's genuinely automated. The strategy fires a signal, the execution layer places the trade, and it lands in your funded account. You don't place a single trade by hand.

What if I lose money or blow an account?

It can happen, and the course addresses it directly. You'll learn the risk settings and account protections that keep losses contained, and why losing days are a normal part of the process rather than a failure.

My story

The whole
honest story.

About five years ago, I Googled one thing.

I fell down a rabbit hole. Ended up on Reddit. And almost everyone said the same thing. Trading.

So I thought, alright. Let's try it. The work I was doing at the time just wasn't pushing me anymore. I needed something that would actually challenge me. Trading felt like that thing.

I found a German trader, Willem Weise, joined his community, and started learning to trade manually. While I was in there, I built a free trading journal in Notion for the whole community. Just because I saw the need for it.

And while I was building that, I noticed something. There was so much potential in automating this. People were doing it by hand. Emotionally. Inconsistently. There had to be a better way.

So I bought my first bot. MT5, from someone in the community. Then I spent weeks, honestly weeks, figuring out the right settings. Testing, tweaking, adjusting. And then it started working. Actually profitable, for a few weeks straight.

Then Trump got elected.

And my profits were gone. Overnight.

But I'd seen it work. That was the moment it clicked: manual trading wasn't for me. Too emotional. And I was travelling full time, dealing with personal stuff. I couldn't sit at a screen all day. I needed something that ran without me.

That's when I found this. Algorithmic trading on prop firms. The algo does the work. The prop firm provides the capital. I just let it run.

And once it's set up and working, it genuinely runs itself. It would have taken me far less time if someone had just laid it all out clearly from the start.

So that's what I'm doing here.
