§ 02 · The reason

Where the brand stopped.

I spend a lot of time at Island Sports Club Cemagi, even when I'm not playing. The tournament screen has been bothering me for months — especially when I score high and want to take a picture of the results to send to friends.

Everything else at ISC is intentional. The palette, the typography, the signage at the courts, the menus, the booking site I came in through. The tournament app on the TV wasn't. You don't need a designer's eye to feel the gap.

I study design. I like to build things I want to exist. So I rebuilt the one thing in the club that was bugging me — in the club's own brand, on a single screen the staff mirror from a phone to the TV.

Same flow they already run. Just looking like the rest of the club.

§ what the new screen gets you

Five small things, one bigger one.

None of these are technical wins. They're the human ones — the moments players talk about long after the match is over.

  • Players want to photograph the results — and they actually post them
  • The club's brand is on the biggest screen in the room, not just at the door
  • Scores readable from across the floor — fewer "what round are we on?" interruptions
  • The champions reveal makes the end of the event a moment, not an afterthought
  • Same iPad or phone the staff already use — no new hardware, no subscription, nothing to renew

That's the whole pitch.

§ 04 · The features

Five screens, one flow.

N° 02
Tab 2 · Rounds · the hero view

Live scoring.

The screen that gets mirrored to the TV. One row per court, oversized numerals, plus and minus buttons, tap-to-open numpad. Drop into a court, drop out — scores stay where you left them.

  • One row per court — Team A · Score · Team B
  • Tap any score to open a full numpad
  • Points mode auto-fills the opposing team
  • Tournament progress bar with one tick per round
  • "Started · Ends · ~ min per round" footer ticking live
  • Previous / Next never loses a prior round's scores
N° 01
Tab 1 · Americano

Tournament setup.

Pick the format, partners, target, player count and end time. Everything else is computed for you.

  • Three formats: Americano · Argentino · King of the Court
  • Rotating or fixed partners
  • 4–24 players · 1–6 courts (linked)
  • "Other" pill on every preset for custom values
  • Live preview of round one's pairings
N° 03
Tab 3 · Timer

Round clock.

A giant ring around the round timer. The last thirty seconds turn warm as a glanceable low-time warning.

  • Length picker: 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 — or any custom value
  • Length locks once the round has started
  • Start · Pause · Resume · Reset · End round
  • Big numerals readable from across the club
N° 04
Tab 4 · Score

Live standings.

A running leaderboard that updates as scores come in. Sorted by total points, with rank, played, won and point-diff at a glance.

  • Top three get accent avatars and a star prefix
  • Diff coloring runs teal for +, warm for −
  • Built for big-screen mirroring at any size
N° 05
Auto view · Champions

The reveal.

Triggered automatically when the last round is confirmed. The console takes over the screen for the big moment.

  • Podium row: 2nd · 1st · 3rd, with medals + per-place stats
  • Final standings list for everyone else, in two columns
  • Soft confetti petals layered behind
  • "Back to standings" or "Start new tournament" to close
§ 05 · Colophon

The look & feel.

Built on the club's own palette.

The console borrows directly from iscbali.com — the same deep teal, the same crisp white, paired with a single marigold accent that picks up the warmth of the Cemagi sunset. Setimo runs through every editorial moment, with League Spartan for the body and JetBrains Mono for numerals.

The result: when staff open the console on the floor and mirror it to the TV, every player feels like they're inside the same brand they booked through — whichever of the four clubs they're in.
