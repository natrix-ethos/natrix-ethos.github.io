# Natrix Ethos — Legal documents

Four policy documents drafted for the Whop "Legal" section:

- `terms-of-service.md`
- `privacy-policy.md`
- `return-policy.md`
- `eula.md`

## Before you publish — fill these in

Every document uses bracketed placeholders. Search for `[` across all four
files and replace:

| Placeholder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `[LEGAL NAME]` | The name the business trades under legally (your full name if sole trader, or the company name) |
| `[LEGAL FORM]` | e.g. sole trader / Einzelunternehmen / GmbH / Ltd |
| `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]` | The address a customer/regulator can serve notice to |
| `[COUNTRY]` | Country of establishment (governs which consumer + data law applies) |
| `[GOVERNING LAW]` | e.g. Germany / the laws of Germany |
| `[COURTS/CITY]` | Competent court location |
| `[CONTACT EMAIL]` | A monitored support/legal inbox |
| `[VAT STATUS]` | VAT-registered? number? or small-business exempt |
| `[EFFECTIVE DATE]` | The date you publish these |

## Important — read this

These are **carefully-built templates, not legal advice**, and I am not a
substitute for a qualified lawyer admitted in your jurisdiction. Trading
education sits in a heightened-scrutiny area (financial promotions rules,
consumer-protection regulators, prop-firm terms). Before you go live, have a
lawyer licensed in Georgia review these — especially the risk disclaimers in
the Terms and the withdrawal-waiver mechanics in the Return Policy.

Whop is the platform you sell through; in many flows **Whop (or a Whop entity)
is the merchant of record and payment processor**, and its own Terms, Privacy
Policy and refund handling sit on top of yours. Nothing here overrides Whop's
platform terms — these documents govern *your* relationship with the customer
regarding *your* course and community.
