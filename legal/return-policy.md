## 2. EU/UK right of withdrawal and its waiver

2.1 If you are a consumer in the EU (or a comparable jurisdiction such as the
UK), you normally have a **14-day right to withdraw** from a distance purchase
without giving a reason.

2.2 For **digital content that is supplied immediately**, this right can be
**lost** once supply has begun, if before purchase you: (a) give your **express
consent** to immediate access, and (b) **acknowledge that you thereby lose your
right of withdrawal**.

2.3 At checkout you will be asked to confirm exactly that. **By requesting
immediate access to the Course and confirming that acknowledgement, you agree
that supply begins at once and you lose the 14-day right of withdrawal.** If you
do **not** give that consent, the ordinary 14-day right applies until access is
provided.

3.1 **Duplicate or accidental charges, or failure to deliver access** — if you

3.2 **Technical issues** — if a technical problem on our side prevents you from

3.3 **Change of mind** — because the Course is digital content that is fully
